You are Director of the Authority. You do not fight, rule, or quest. You lay track, buy engines, and answer correspondence.

A Delve produces iron and cannot grow food. A Shire grows food and has no tools. Neither can do anything about this without you.

Everything that happens to the realm afterwards is, technically, your fault.
