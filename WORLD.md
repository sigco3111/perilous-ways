# The Gazetteer

The realm is **Eärdon**. It is not Middle-earth, but it has read Middle-earth and
taken notes. Halflings, Elves, Dwarves, Men, Orcs, Ents, wizards, wargs, trolls,
barrow-wights and at least one dragon are all present and accounted for.

The register is **half parody, half straight**. The Elves sailing west should
actually land as sad. The halfling complaining about the whistle frightening her
hens should actually be funny. Neither should undercut the other — that balance
is the whole trick, and it lives in the writing.

---

## Peoples

| Culture | Settlement kind | In their own account | In the Authority's files |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Halflings** | `shire` | the Little Folk of the Greenhollow | "Ratepayers, Class III (Compliant)" |
| **Dwarves** | `delve` | the Khazadrim of the Deeps | "Contractors (Preferred)" |
| **Elves** | `haven` | the Eldest, the Firstborn | "Objectors, Standing" |
| **Men** | `burg` | the Marchfolk, subjects of the King | "The Crown; also the Council" |
| **Orcs** | `hold` | — | "The Eastern Party" |

Notes that matter mechanically:

- **Halflings** are the tax base and the moral centre. Comfortable, parochial,
  enormous consumers of ale and grain. They write letters. They write so many
  letters.
- **Dwarves** are the engineers. Their regard unlocks tunnelling, metallurgy, and
  better engines. They regard a tunnel as a work of art and will bore one
  through anything for money.
- **Elves** are the conscience and the cost. Regard falls with every acre of
  felled wood and every smelter. At zero they take ship from the Grey Quays and
  do not come back: their havens go dark, Lore leaves the economy for good, and
  the map gets measurably less beautiful. This should hurt.
- **Men** are the Council. They set the grant and the mandate, and they will
  cheerfully ask you to do two contradictory things in the same week.
- **Orcs** are, in Age II, the single most profitable market on the continent.
  They pay in Ashglass and Iron. They are also appalling passengers, and every
  crate of freight moved east raises the Shadow. The Authority's position is that
  a paying customer is a paying customer.

## Regions, west to east

- **The Greenhollow** — halfling country. Downs, hedges, farmland, six kinds of
  hill. Peril: essentially zero, which the Authority notes with suspicion.
- **The Grey Quays** — the western coast and estuary. Elvish. Ships leave from
  here. Gulls.
- **The Barrowdowns** — bare chalk ridges south of the Greenhollow, full of
  tombs. Peril: moderate and *nocturnal*. Wights object to earthworks.
- **Elderwood** — the Elves' great wood, and **Lorindel** within it.
- **Brackenford** — the crossroads. Everything passes through here eventually,
  which makes it the natural first interchange and the natural first disaster.
- **The Marchlands** — the plains and river country of Men. **Amonhal** is the
  King's city.
- **Mirkholt** — dark, old, spidered forest in the north. Peril: high.
- **Wodenholt** — the Ent wood. Cannot be felled. Attempting to route through it
  produces the slowest legal dispute in recorded history.
- **The Sundering Peaks** — the mountain wall that splits the continent in two.
  Two passes, both bad. **Dhurunzol** is the great Dwarf-delve beneath it.
- **The Frostmarch** — snow highlands in the far north.
- **The Weeping Marsh** — a fen in the south centre. Sinks things.
- **The Ashenreach** — the east. Ash plain, slag, orc-holds, and **Mount Wrack**.
  Peril: severe. Profit: extraordinary.

## Persons of note

- **Vurmath the Grey**, later **Vurmath the White** (self-promoted). A wizard,
  and the Authority's most enthusiastic stakeholder. He wants more track, more
  coal, more smelters, and a seat on the Board. He is right about everything
  except the important part. Arrives Age I as an ally, becomes an Age II problem.
- **Elberic the Grey.** The other wizard. Turns up unannounced to disapprove of
  things and decline refreshment. Occasionally saves the realm and never files
  the paperwork.
- **Mistress Amaranth Puddifoot** of Nether Wembly. Has written eleven times
  about the whistle. Will write again.
- **Thrain Ironmoot**, Master of Works. Believes every problem is a tunnel.
- **The Eastern Party.** Does not correspond. Reads everything.
